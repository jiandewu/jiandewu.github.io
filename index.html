About

I build machine learning and LLM systems on messy real-world data. At the Center for Public Health Modeling and Response (CPHMR), Clemson University, I manage multi-source EHR and CDC surveillance data and forecast respiratory hospitalizations (COVID-19, influenza, RSV); my transformer-based multi-source forecasting work is published in Epidemics (2025). I also build LLM and agent tooling for research workflows: reliability-aware MCP tools over surveillance data, evaluated document retrieval, and automated LLM pipelines that run unattended.

Before that I spent nearly six years at LSU Health Sciences Center building reproducible RNA-seq, DNA methylation, and single-cell workflows, and earned a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the University of New Orleans on GPU and cluster parallel particle filtering.

Now

2026-10: Observation-aware forecasting of respiratory hospitalizations, and LLM tools for surveillance data (resplens).

AI Frontier Digest

Automated daily AI-news digest (in Chinese). Collects 31 researcher, lab, and news feeds plus Hacker News and Hugging Face Daily Papers; an LLM summarizes, scores importance, and groups items covering the same event; selected items get full-text explanations. GitHub Actions publishes every 4 hours.
