About

I work on public health data and infectious disease forecasting at the Center for Public Health Modeling and Response (CPHMR), Clemson University. My current focus is forecasting respiratory hospitalizations (COVID-19, influenza, RSV) from CDC surveillance data while accounting for reporting gaps and data-quality variation.

From 2017 to 2023 I was a Senior Bioinformatics Specialist at LSU Health Sciences Center, building reproducible workflows for RNA-seq, DNA methylation, and single-cell analyses of cancer cohorts. I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at the University of New Orleans, where I worked on parallel and GPU-accelerated particle filtering for target tracking.
